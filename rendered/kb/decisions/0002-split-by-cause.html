0002 · Before a ticket is marked known, split it by cause

owner support desk proposed reviewed 2026-11-11 · 0 days ago

Date: 11 November 2026 · Proposed by: the author of #4471 split · To decide: Rune (product), support desk

Proposed: keep merging by subject at intake (decision 0001), but before a merged ticket is marked known, read its reports for which fix would stop each one and split it by cause. 0001 stays accepted until this one is decided.

Context

0001 merges reports whose subject lines match, without reading the body. It accepted one cost: reports with different subjects about the same bug end up in separate tickets, merged later.

#4471 showed the opposite cost, which 0001 does not name: reports with the same subject from different bugs end up in one ticket. “Invite came on the wrong day” was written about the parser bug and about the booking-v3 hint bug alike. The ticket was marked known with one fix queued, and two of its three problems had nobody working on them (#4471 split).

Decision (proposed)

  1. Intake keeps merging by subject — 0001’s reason, speed during an incident, still holds.
  2. Before a merged ticket’s status becomes known, its reports are placed by what their bodies say (#lib/kb/symptoms) and grouped by the fix that would stop them.
  3. One problem, one ticket; the incident page shows every problem with its ticket and its count.

Consequences

  • A big ticket costs one read of its bodies before it is marked known — minutes with place(), not a person’s morning.
  • Two bugs can no longer hide in one known ticket.
  • Reports that name no date are placed last, by inference, and labelled as such.
  • If accepted, 0001 becomes amended (intake rule kept, “known” rule added); 0001’s text stays as written.
