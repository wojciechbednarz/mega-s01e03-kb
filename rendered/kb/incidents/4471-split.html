#4471 split · two causes, not one

owner support desk split reviewed 2026-11-11 · 0 days ago

#4471 holds two causes, not one. Every report in it is about a wrong date, so they were merged as one issue — but by the desk’s own rule (causes.ts) they come from two causes that need two fixes, so each cause has a ticket of its own: parser and hint.

A cause is decided by where a report comes from — the tenant’s locale, its booking form and the channel the report came through — not by its subject, and not by intake’s tag (causes.ts). Same subject, different cause is normal here.

5 of the parser reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json); counted where they're filed, footnoted here.

  • parser — the shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s dates come out swapped. This is what the #4471 page describes. One fix: read dates in the tenant’s order.
  • hint — booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field; people at day-first tenants type what it says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Only reports that came through the form from a booking-v3 day-first tenant. Fixing the parser does not fix these.
  • The desk’s one-day notes — the desk read a few parser reports as a date moving by one day (desk-notes.json). As the desk does, they are counted where they’re filed and footnoted above; whether they are a cause of their own is a task, not a ticket yet.

Why one ticket held two causes

  • Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match, without reading anything else; the triage page says “when unsure, merge”. Both causes share subjects like “Invite came on the wrong day” (see the table).
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the browser: some hint reports were tagged parser, and the other way round.

Decision 0001 stays as accepted (decisions are never edited). What it should change is proposed in decision 0002.
