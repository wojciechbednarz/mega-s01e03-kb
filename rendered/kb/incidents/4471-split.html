#4471 split · one cause merged in by mistake

owner support desk split reviewed 2026-11-12 · 0 days ago

#4471 holds two causes, not one. Every report in it is about a wrong date, so they were merged as one issue. By the desk’s own rule (causes.ts) a ticket is the cause most of its reports share, and the rest were merged into the wrong place: #4471 is the parser ticket, and the hint reports were merged into it by mistake. They need a different fix, so they now have a ticket of their own, issue #4.

A cause is decided by where a report comes from — the tenant’s locale, its booking form and the channel the report came through — not by its subject, and not by intake’s tag (causes.ts). Same subject, different cause is normal here.

5 of the parser reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json); counted where they're filed, footnoted here.

  • parser — the shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s dates come out swapped. This is what the #4471 page describes, and #4471 stays its ticket. One fix: read dates in the tenant’s order.
  • hint — booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field; people at day-first tenants type what it says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Only reports that came through the form from a booking-v3 day-first tenant. Fixing the parser does not fix these. Their ticket: issue #4.
  • The desk’s one-day notes — the desk read a few reports as a date moving by one day (desk-notes.json). By the desk’s rule (causes.ts) they are parser reports: they stay in #4471, footnoted there. A task checks that the parser fix clears them too.

Why one ticket held two causes

  • Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match, without reading anything else; the triage page says “when unsure, merge”. Both causes share subjects like “Invite came on the wrong day” (see the table).
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the browser: some hint reports were tagged parser, and the other way round.

Decision 0001 stays as accepted (decisions are never edited). What it should change is proposed in decision 0002.
