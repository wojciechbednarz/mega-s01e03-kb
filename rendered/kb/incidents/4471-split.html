#4471 split · three problems, not one

owner support desk split reviewed 2026-11-11 · 0 days ago

#4471 holds three problems, not one. Every one of its reports is about a wrong date, so they were merged as one issue. But they need three different fixes, so each problem has a ticket of its own: parser, hint and time-zone.

Read each report for which fix would stop it, not for “it’s about dates”. A swapped day and month (7 April → 4 July) is a months-long jump; a time-zone error moves a date by hours, at most one day. One bug can’t do both.

4 reports name no date and were placed by inference from same-tenant, same-channel reports; which ones, and why, is in each ticket.

  • parser — the shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s dates come out swapped. This is what the #4471 page describes. One fix: read dates in the tenant’s order.
  • hint — the same swap, but the parser is not the cause: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type what it says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Only reports that came through the form from a booking-v3 day-first tenant. Fixing the parser does not fix these.
  • time-zone — the date moves by one day, it isn’t swapped (“Tuesday 12 May… Monday 11 May”, “Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”). A different fix again: time zones and daylight saving.

How every report was placed

Strongest evidence first, stop at the first that answers (#lib/kb/symptoms):

  1. The text. The dates the reporter wrote: swapped, or one day apart. Proof.
  2. The desk’s note. Reports the desk already read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json).
  3. Inferred, last. A report whose body names no date takes the symptom of its siblings — the same tenant through the same channel — when at least 5 are placed and 90% agree. It describes the customer, not the report, so it runs last and is labelled. Hint can’t be inferred for a report that didn’t come through the form.

Why one ticket held three problems

  • Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match, without reading the body; the triage page says “when unsure, merge”. Different bugs share subjects like “Wrong day on the invite”.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the browser: some hint reports were tagged parser.
  • Grouping by tenant isn’t enough either: the desk’s one-day moves came from parser tenants.

Decision 0001 stays as accepted (decisions are never edited). What it should change is proposed in decision 0002.
